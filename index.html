Welcome to Digitalis Survivor

You are the on‑call clinical pharmacist. Keep all three patients stable for 5 days. Each day you must set doses, correct electrolytes, and respond to events.

  • Therapeutic levels are usually ~0.5–2.0 ng/mL; levels > 2.0 raise risk, and > 3.0 suggest toxicity. Always interpret with clinical context and electrolytes.
  • Hypokalemia / hypomagnesemia increase digoxin sensitivity; renal failure slows clearance; hypercalcemia may potentiate toxicity.
  • In severe toxicity, consider digoxin‑immune Fab.
Tip: Correct K⁺/Mg²⁺ before increasing the dose.
